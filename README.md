# Rink Board

A hockey coaching whiteboard for the phone, iPad, and PC. It runs in Chrome. Safari works too.

On this PC, double-click `index.html`. It opens in Chrome.

On your phone or iPad, open https://dtiberio6.github.io/hockey-board/ in Chrome. Add it to the Home Screen if you want it one tap away. After it has loaded once, it still opens if the rink Wi-Fi drops.

## On the ice

The phone stays in landscape. If you are holding it upright, turn it sideways. An iPad works upright or sideways.

The rink view is the dropdown. Half ends on the center line. Zone ends on the near blue line. 3/4 ends on the far blue line. Neutral is the ice between the inside edges of the end faceoff circles. Stations is the end zone with a gray divider from the boards to the blue line. Each view stays centered on the page. Flip swaps which end is on the left.

On a phone, the tool sections sit on the left: teams, label set, pieces, actions, draw, and edit. The name, view, and file buttons stay along the bottom. On a computer, that whole panel is on the left. Draw uses the color you pick, and the palette is a short row of small chips.

Three label sets. Pos is G, LD, RD, LW, C, RW. Unit is G, D1, D2, F1, F2, F3. XO is X and O for drill markings, and you can place as many as you need. Named players are one per team: a second click moves the one already on the ice. Defense get a dark ring. Both goalies are gray.

Home and Away are solid colors you can tell apart. Tap a team to place it. Tap the active team again to change its color. The mouse wheel over the ice changes which player is armed, and the marker follows the cursor until you click.

Drop a puck on a player, or drag one onto a player, and it stays with them. That player gets a yellow ring. Only one puck is assigned. The rest, and a pile of pucks, sit loose. Right-click a player and choose Assign puck to move the assigned puck to them. A pass moves it to the player at the end of the line. A shot puts it in the net.

Skate, Carry, Back, Pass, and Shot follow the color of the team that makes the play. Back is backwards skating, drawn as alternating parentheses. A shot is a double line. Draw still uses the color chips. Right-click and drag moves a piece. Right-click without dragging asks Erase?, and a net can be flipped from that menu.

Select a piece or a line, then Delete. Erase removes whatever you tap, one after another. Clear asks you to tap again so a miss does not wipe the drill. Undo brings it back.

Pinch to zoom. Two fingers pan. One finger draws or places. Right-drag moves a piece.

## Drills

Save stores the drill on that device. Drills, then a name, opens it again. Export writes a file you can send to your other device. Import reads it back. Image saves a picture of the ice for a group text.

Notes hold the setup, the cues, and the rotation. They stay with the drill.
